## Overview

The `FraudCheckFailed` event is published when the accumulated risk score meets or exceeds the configured threshold. It carries the payment metadata, the percentage score, and a `declineReason` describing the strongest fraud signal that triggered the rejection so downstream services can log, alert, or display the justification.

## Decline Reasons

- `HIGH_RISK_SCORE`: Payment amount or velocity pushed the score over the threshold.
- `BLOCKLISTED`: The customer is on a blocklist that causes an immediate rejection.
- `VELOCITY`: Too many payments in a short window.
- `MISMATCH`: Currency or location does not match the customer profile.
