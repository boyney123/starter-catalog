## Overview

`CustomerEmailVerified` is published by the [[service|customer-api]] whenever a customer completes the email verification flow. Downstream systems can consume this event to unlock features that require a verified email address, such as order confirmation notifications or account recovery.

The event is published on the Kafka topic `customers.customer-email-verified.v1`.
