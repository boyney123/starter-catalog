## Overview

`CustomerClosed` is published by the [[service|customer-api]] when a customer's account is closed. The closure is idempotent — closing an already-closed account does nothing and no second event is emitted.

Consumers of this event can use it to react to account closures, for example by anonymising customer data, cancelling active subscriptions, or releasing reserved resources.

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| `eventId` | Unique identifier (UUID v4) for this event instance. |
| `occurredAt` | ISO 8601 timestamp of when the account was closed. |
| `customerId` | UUID of the customer whose account was closed. |
| `reason` | Why the account was closed: `CUSTOMER_REQUEST`, `FRAUD`, or `INACTIVITY`. |
