## Overview

The **Review System** owns customer reviews end to end. The [[service|review-api]] accepts new reviews, votes and flags and stores them in the [[container|review-database]]. The [[service|review-moderation-worker]] moderates each submitted review and records the decision. The [[service|rating-aggregator]] turns published reviews into the star ratings shown on the storefront, cached in the [[container|rating-cache]].
